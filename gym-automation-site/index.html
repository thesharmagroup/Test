Kya milega

4 automations jo aapke gym ka roz ka kaam sambhal lengi

Google Sheet aapka database hai, n8n kaam chalata hai, aur WhatsApp member tak message pahunchata hai.

01 · MEMBER ENTRY

Naya member khud Sheet mein

Front desk par tablet ya phone par ek simple form. Member ki details bharo, baaki system khud karta hai.

  • Plan se expiry date apne aap calculate (month-end bhi sahi)
  • Phone number khud 91 format mein
  • Same number dobara aaye to duplicate entry nahi
Form→ Calculate→ Google Sheet
02 · EXPIRY REMINDER

Expiry se pehle WhatsApp

Roz subah system Sheet check karta hai aur jin members ki membership khatam hone wali hai, unhe WhatsApp par reminder bhejta hai.

  • 3 din pehle aur expiry wale din reminder
  • Ek din mein ek member ko ek hi message
  • Sirf un members ko jinhone WhatsApp ke liye haan kaha ho
Roz 9 AM→ Sheet check→ WhatsApp
03 · PAYMENT UPDATE

Payment aate hi status Paid

Member online pay kare (Razorpay link) ya cash/UPI de: status apne aap update hota hai aur nayi expiry date set ho jaati hai.

  • Online: Razorpay payment link se automatic
  • Cash/UPI: owner sirf ek chhota form bharta hai
  • Ek payment do baar count nahi hoti
Payment→ Verify→ Paid + nayi expiry
04 · DAILY REPORT

Roz subah owner ko report

Active, expired aur pending payments ki ginti, naam ke saath, aapke email ya WhatsApp par. Sheet kholne ki zaroorat nahi.

  • Report ka time aapki pasand se
  • Email mein poori list, WhatsApp par short summary
  • Sheet mein Dashboard tab bhi milega
Roz 8 AM→ Ginti→ Email / WhatsApp

FAQ

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kya mujhe computer ya coding aani chahiye?

Nahi. Aapko bas Google Sheet kholna aana chahiye, aur woh phone par bhi khulti hai. Saara technical setup hum karte hain aur 30 minute ki training dete hain.

Mere purane members ka kya hoga?

Purana Excel ya register ka data ek baar Sheet mein daal diya jaata hai. Uske baad naye members form se apne aap judte hain.

WhatsApp messages ka kitna kharch aayega?

Meta har delivered template message ka charge leta hai. Reminder "utility" category mein aate hain, jiska rate kam hai. Example: 130 members wale gym mein mahine ke 80 reminder lagbhag ₹9 + GST ke aaspaas pad sakte hain. Rates badalte rehte hain, isliye final rate Meta ke official rate card se confirm karte hain.

Setup mein kitna time lagta hai?

Aapke accounts aur data ready hone ke baad kuch din. WhatsApp Business verification aur template approval mein Meta ki taraf se kuch din lag sakte hain, jo humare control mein nahi hai; yeh hum shuru mein hi bata dete hain.

Member cash ya UPI se pay kare to?

Koi dikkat nahi. Aapko ek chhota "Record Payment" form milega: phone, amount aur mode bharo, status aur expiry apne aap update.

Kya yeh system mere naam par rahega?

Haan. Google, n8n aur WhatsApp ke accounts aapke naam par bante hain aur handover document mein saari details milti hain. Hum chale bhi jaayein to system aapka hi rahega.

Kya isse pakka zyada renewals aayengi?

Koi guarantee nahi de sakta. System yeh pakka karta hai ki koi member reminder ke bina na chhoote, aur aapko roz saaf hisaab mile. Renewal phir bhi member ka faisla hai.
