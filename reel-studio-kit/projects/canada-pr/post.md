# canada-pr: Instagram post

## Caption

Ontario mein PR ka seedha rasta band ho gaya? 🇨🇦

30 May 2026 ko OINP ke saare 9 streams band hue, Masters aur PhD graduate streams bhi.
Naye streams aa rahe hain, par details abhi confirm nahi.
5 Nov ke naye plan mein PR target 2029 tak 3,80,000 per saal hi hai, aur naye students sirf 1,50,000 per saal.
Achhi khabar: 2027 mein Canada mein kaam kar rahe workers ke liye ek time-limited PR measure aayega.

Status expire hone se pehle options check karo, aur sirf licensed consultant (RCIC) se salah lo. Ye legal advice nahi hai.

Aise updates ke liye follow karo @adityaak_47
