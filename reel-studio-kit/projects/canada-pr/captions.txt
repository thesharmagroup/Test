Ontario mein PR ka seedha rasta band ho gaya hai. 30 May 2026 ko Ontario ne apne PR program, OINP, ke saare 9 streams band kar diye. Isme Masters aur PhD graduate streams bhi the, jo Ontario se padhe students ke liye the. Naye streams aa rahe hain, focus job offer par hai, par puri details abhi confirm nahi hain. Ontario ko 2026 ke liye 14,119 nominations mile hain. Aur 5 November ko Canada ka naya plan aaya. PR target 2029 tak 3 lakh 80 hazaar par hi rahega. Naye students sirf 1 lakh 50 hazaar per saal. Goal hai, temporary residents ko population ke 5 percent se neeche laana. Matlab, jinke work permit khatam ho rahe hain, unke liye race aur tight hai. Lekin ek achhi khabar bhi hai. 2027 mein Canada mein kaam kar rahe workers ke liye ek time-limited PR measure aayega, details abhi aani hain. Toh agar aap Ontario mein ho, status expire hone se pehle options check karo, aur sirf licensed consultant se salah lo. Aise updates ke liye follow karo.
