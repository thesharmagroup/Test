## Facts and sources

- College/non-university programs: CLB 5 and the field-of-study requirement (study permit applied on/after 1 Nov 2024); "There are no field of study requirements for graduates with a bachelor's degree, master's degree or doctoral degree" (CLB 7 for degrees). IRCC PGWP eligibility (page date 2026-06-24): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html
- Field list: "you must graduate from a program linked to certain occupations in long-term shortage"; "For 2026, we won't add or remove any eligible fields of study." Listed: 19.0708 Child care and support services management, 19.0709 Child care provider/assistant (Education); 51.2601 Health aide, 51.3902 Nursing assistant/aide and patient care assistant/aide (Healthcare and social services). Business administration (52.0201), accounting (52.0301) and business/commerce general (52.0101) not found. https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/field-of-study/currently-eligible.html
- PGWP length: program of 2 years or more gives a PGWP "valid for 3 years". https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/about.html
- Express Entry categories (page date 2026-10-16): "Early childhood educators and assistants | 42202" in Education; "Nurse aides, orderlies and patient service associates | 33102" in Healthcare and social services; at least 12 months full-time experience in the past 3 years. https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/rounds-invitations/category-based-selection.html
- Ontario: all nine OINP streams revoked effective 30 May 2026. KPMG GMS Flash Alert 2026-139: https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-139.html
- Saskatchewan: healthcare is a priority sector; Student category for Saskatchewan graduates with a PGWP who have worked in Saskatchewan at least six months. https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/immigration-faqs and .../assess-your-eligibility
- Atlantic Immigration Program: "You must receive a job offer from a designated employer in Atlantic Canada". https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration.html

Not verified, left out: Nova Scotia "International Graduates in Demand" (page redirected), Manitoba International Education Stream (page blocked), "PSW" as an exact program title on the IRCC list (the list says "Health aide" and "Nursing assistant/aide"), supply chain diploma (only seen in a summary).
