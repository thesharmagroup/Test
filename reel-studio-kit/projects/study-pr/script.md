Language: Hinglish (spoken and on screen). Style: paper (cream grid, red), standing presenter, photos, background music, SFX. CTA: follow @adityaak_47.

## Hook (max 3 s)

"Hotel front desk ka experience hai? Toh do plan saath chalao."
Shown: hotel card, pill "Plan A + B".

## Beats

1. "Pehle sach: front desk TEER 4 job hai, isliye Express Entry ye experience nahi maanta." Shown: NOC 64314 = TEER 4, stamp "EE: NO".
2. "Plan A: Atlantic Immigration Program. Nova Scotia ya New Brunswick mein designated employer se hotel ki permanent, full-time job offer lo." Shown: Halifax photo, Plan A card.
3. "Chahiye sirf CLB 4 English, 12th ka ECA, aur pichle 5 saal mein 1 saal ka experience." Shown: checklist.
4. "Plan B: job offer na mile, toh 2 saal ka diploma karo. Pehli choice health care aide, job nurse aide ki, Express Entry ki healthcare category. Doosri choice Early Childhood Education." Shown: hospital photo, #1 / #2.
5. "Business diploma mat lo, uspe PGWP nahi milta." Shown: stamp "NO PGWP".
6. "Padhai Nova Scotia, New Brunswick ya Saskatchewan mein karo. Ontario ke PR streams May 2026 mein band ho gaye." Shown: province list, Ontario stamp "BAND".

## Twist

"2 saal ke program par 3 saal ka PGWP, phir 12 mahine full-time kaam."
Shown: 2 → 3 saal, 12 mahine count.

## CTA

## Facts and sources

- NOC 64314 "Hotel front desk clerks", TEER 4 (Alberta eligible-occupations table): https://www.alberta.ca/tourism-and-hospitality-stream-eligibility
- Express Entry: FSW needs "1 of these TEER categories: 0, 1, 2, or 3" (page date 2026-11-03): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/who-can-apply/federal-skilled-workers.html ; CEC needs "TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3": https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/who-can-apply/canadian-experience-class.html
- AIP status "Open" (page date 2026-06-22): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration/how-to-immigrate.html
- AIP job offer: designated employer in Atlantic Canada; TEER 4 "for permanent employment with no set end date", full-time "at least 30 hours a week" (2026-09-22): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration/how-to-immigrate/job-offer.html
- AIP language: "CLB 4 for job offer in TEER 4" (2026-05-28): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration/language-testing.html
- AIP education: TEER 4 needs "the foreign equivalent of a Canadian high school diploma (or higher)" with an ECA (2026-06-22): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration/education-assessment.html
- AIP experience: "at least 1,560 hours of related work experience over the past 5 years", "over a period of at least 1 year", same or higher TEER as the job offer, paid, not self-employed (2026-05-28): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration/how-to-immigrate/work-experience.html . NOTE: the page does not explicitly say that work outside Canada counts; it only adds a work-permit rule "If the job was in Canada". Whether the India experience counts should be confirmed with an RCIC.
- Ontario: all nine OINP streams revoked effective 30 May 2026 (KPMG GMS Flash Alert 2026-139): https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-139.html
- Saskatchewan: Student category for Saskatchewan graduates with a PGWP who have worked in Saskatchewan at least six months; healthcare is a priority sector: https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/assess-your-eligibility
- (Not in the spoken script any more) Alberta Tourism and Hospitality Stream: current full-time work for an Alberta tourism/hospitality employer, LMIA-based work permit, "a minimum of 6 consecutive months (or minimum of 780 hours)", CLB 4, lists 64314: https://www.alberta.ca/tourism-and-hospitality-stream-eligibility
- PGWP: college programs need an eligible field (study permit on/after 1 Nov 2024); business administration/accounting/business-commerce codes not found on the list; Child care (19.0708, 19.0709) and Health aide (51.2601), Nursing assistant/aide (51.3902) listed: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/field-of-study/currently-eligible.html ; 2-year program gives a PGWP "valid for 3 years": https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/about.html
- Express Entry categories: NOC 42202 Early childhood educators and assistants (Education), NOC 33102 Nurse aides, orderlies and patient service associates (Healthcare and social services), 12 months experience in past 3 years (2026-10-16): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/rounds-invitations/category-based-selection.html
