12th ke baad Ontario mein ECE lein ya PSW? Pehle ek baat: aapka 1 saal ka front desk experience TEER 4 hai, Express Entry mein count nahi hoga. ECE yaani Early Childhood Education, 2 saal ka Ontario College Diploma. Isse 3 saal ka PGWP milta hai. Job early childhood educator ki, jo Express Entry ki education category mein hai. PSW yaani Personal Support Worker, sirf 1 saal ka certificate. Centennial mein international fees lagbhag 18,000 dollar. Lekin PGWP bhi program jitna, yaani lagbhag 1 saal. Job hospital ya long-term care mein lo, wahan PSW NOC 33102 hai, healthcare category. Express Entry ke liye 12 mahine full-time kaam chahiye. Ontario ka naya Workforce Priority stream bhi hai: job offer, usi job mein 6 mahine, aur CLB 6 English. Dhyan rakho, public-private partnership campus par PGWP nahi milta. Toh lamba aur safe rasta ECE, jaldi aur sasta PSW. Admission se pehle college ka PGWP status zaroor check karo.
