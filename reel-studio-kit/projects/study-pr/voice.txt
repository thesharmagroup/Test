Hotel front desk ka experience hai? Toh PR ka ye rasta dekho. Pehle sach: front desk TEER 4 job hai, aur Express Entry TEER 4 experience nahi maanta. Isliye sabse seedha rasta hai Atlantic Immigration Program, jo abhi open hai. Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, PEI ya Newfoundland mein designated employer se hotel ki permanent, full-time job offer chahiye. TEER 4 job ke liye sirf CLB 4 English, 12th ka ECA, aur pichle 5 saal mein 1 saal ka experience. Aapke paas 3 saal hai. Alberta ka Tourism and Hospitality stream bhi front desk leta hai, par wahan pehle 6 mahine kaam karna padta hai. Aur padhai se jaana hai? Business diploma mat lo, uspe PGWP nahi milta. Early Childhood Education ya health aide lo. Ye Express Entry categories mein hain, aur 2 saal ke program par 3 saal ka PGWP. Paise dekar job offer mat khareedo. PR ki guarantee koi nahi deta. Aise updates ke liye follow karo.
