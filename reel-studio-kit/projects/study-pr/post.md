## Caption

12th ke baad Ontario mein ECE lein ya PSW? 🇨🇦
ECE: 2 saal ka Ontario College Diploma, 3 saal ka PGWP, job Express Entry ki education category (NOC 42202).
PSW: 1 saal ka certificate, sasta aur jaldi, par PGWP bhi lagbhag 1 saal. Job hospital/long-term care mein (NOC 33102, healthcare category).
Hotel front desk ka experience (TEER 4) Express Entry mein count nahi hota.
Ontario Workforce Priority stream: job offer, usi job mein 6 mahine, CLB 6.
Public-private partnership campus par PGWP nahi milta. Admission se pehle college ka PGWP status aur CIP code check karo.
Ye legal advice nahi hai. Info 10 Nov 2026 tak ki hai.
