# recharge-mehnga

Topic: Recharge itna mehnga kyun hua?
Language: Hinglish (spoken and on screen)

## Hook (max 3 s)

"209 rupaye ka recharge ab 249 ka kyun?"
Shown: big "₹209 → ₹249" title card from frame 0.

## Beats

1. "3 July 2024 ko Jio aur Airtel ne plans 10 se 25 percent tak mehnge kar diye." Shown: date sticker, BigNumber counting up to 25%.
2. "Pehli wajah: 5G. Companies ne sirf 5G spectrum par 20 billion dollar se zyada kharch kiye. Ab wo paisa wapas chahiye." Shown: card "Wajah 1: 5G", BigNumber "$20B+".
3. "Doosri wajah: ARPU, yaani ek user se har mahine ki kamai." Shown: card defining ARPU.
4. "Airtel ka ARPU tha lagbhag 210 rupaye, aur company kehti hai ye 300 se upar hona chahiye." Shown: ₹210 vs target ₹300+.
5. "Hike ke baad sirf ek quarter mein ye 211 se 233 ho gaya." Shown: BigNumber count-up 211 → 233, sticker "1 quarter".

## Twist

"Lekin asli twist: ye last hike nahi hai. Analysts ek aur 10 se 15 percent hike ka andaaza laga rahe hain, abhi confirm nahi hua."
Shown: "Next hike?" card with "10-15%" and a "not confirmed" sticker.

## CTA

"Aise aur updates ke liye follow karo."
Shown: end card with FOLLOW and @adityaak_47. (Follow CTA instead of a comment keyword, as asked.)
