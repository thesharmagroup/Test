## Facts and sources

- 2026 allocation total 5,261 (priority and other sectors 3,946; capped sectors 1,315), nominations issued to date; capped-sector positions filled (e.g. Accommodation and Food Service 180 of 180 on 13 Jan 2026). SINP Processing Statistics, Q3 2026: https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/sinp-processing-statistics
- Initial 2026 allocation 4,761; priority sectors at least 50% (healthcare, agriculture, skilled trades, mining, manufacturing, energy, technology); capped sectors max 25% (accommodation and food services, retail trade, trucking); 750 spaces for Saskatchewan graduates; JAFs replaced by EPAs; graduates who studied outside Saskatchewan not eligible under Saskatchewan Experience. SINP Immigration FAQs (updated 19 Dec 2025): https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/immigration-faqs
- Points grid: "at least 60 points", maximum 110. https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/assess-your-eligibility
- Employment Offer: permanent full-time job offer in TEER 0-3, at least one year (1,560 hours) of experience in the last 10 years, CLB 4, $500 fee, PGWP/SOWP holders need six months with the employer, job offer approved by SINP first. https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/browse-sinp-programs/applicants-international-skilled-workers/international-skilled-worker-with-employment-offer
- CLB 4 minimum (IELTS L 4.5, R 3.5, W 4, S 4). https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/live-in-saskatchewan/by-immigrating/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program/language-requirements-for-sinp
- Provincial nomination = 600 CRS points. IRCC CRS criteria: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/check-score/crs-criteria.html
- No scheduled EOI draws (Occupation In-Demand / Express Entry): the official processing statistics page shows no draw data; third-party: https://www.ansariimmigration.com/post/sinp-streams-in-2026-which-ones-are-actually-open-and-which-one-fits-you . Least certain claim in the script.
