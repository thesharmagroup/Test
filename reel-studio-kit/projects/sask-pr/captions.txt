Saskatchewan mein PR ka rasta abhi bhi khula hai. Iska program hai SINP, aur 2026 mein iska quota 5,261 nominations ka hai. Aadhe se zyada seats priority sectors ke liye hain: healthcare, agriculture, trades, mining aur tech. Food service, retail aur trucking par 25 percent ka cap hai, aur unki seats jaldi bhar jaati hain. Sabse seedha rasta hai Saskatchewan employer se full-time job offer. Employer pehle EPA approve karwata hai, phir aap apply karte ho. Chahiye 110 mein se kam se kam 60 points, ek saal ka experience, aur sirf CLB 4 English. PGWP waalon ko usi employer ke saath 6 mahine kaam chahiye. Saskatchewan ke college se padhe ho, toh Student stream bhi hai. Lekin bina job offer wale draws ka abhi koi schedule nahi hai. Aur sabse badi baat, nomination mil gaya toh Express Entry mein seedhe 600 points. Application fee 500 dollar hai. Sirf official site ya licensed consultant pe bharosa karo.
